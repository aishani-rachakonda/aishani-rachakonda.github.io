    PerformaMatch
    Current Sep 2026 – Present

    I'm building the recommendation engine behind PerformaMatch, a platform that helps performing artists find auditions and masterclasses. The hard part is cold start: most artists and opportunities have little or no interaction history. So I represent both as embeddings in a shared space and match them by meaning rather than by clicks. As people use the platform, I'm adding collaborative filtering on signals like views, saves, and applications, so recommendations get more personal over time.
