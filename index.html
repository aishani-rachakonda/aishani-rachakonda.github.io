Experience

PerformaMatch
Sep 2026 – Present

Machine Learning Engineer

I'm building the recommendation engine behind PerformaMatch, a platform that helps performing artists find auditions and masterclasses. The hard part is cold start: most artists and opportunities have little or no interaction history. So I represent both as embeddings in a shared space and match them by meaning rather than by clicks. As people use the platform, I'm adding collaborative filtering on signals like views, saves, and applications, so recommendations get more personal over time.

Recommender Systems · Embeddings · Semantic Search · Collaborative Filtering

Ceragon Networks
Summer 2026

AI/ML Research Engineer Intern

Over the summer I built an agentic AI platform, from early research all the way to a production deployment. Operations teams had huge databases of sensor telemetry, device-health signals, and infrastructure data covering hundreds of industrial assets, and answering a single question could take hours of manual digging. With the platform, they could ask in plain English. Specialized agents found the relevant records, ran the analysis, and returned an answer in seconds. It also gave field crews optimized routes, so they could spend their time on the highest-risk assets instead of sifting through raw data.
